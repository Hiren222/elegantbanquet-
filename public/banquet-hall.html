About Our Venue

An Exquisite Venue on Juhu Tara Road

Located on Juhu Tara Road, at the ground floor of Bloom Hotel in Shivaji Nagar, Juhu, Mumbai, Elegant Banquets Juhu offers an idyllic, centrally cooled destination for life's greatest celebrations.

Our spacious AC hall is carefully designed to accommodate 300 seated or up to 500 floating guests in supreme comfort, surrounded by grand crystal chandeliers and regal stage arrangements.

From traditional Weddings, vibrant Haldi and Mehendi ceremonies to energetic Cocktail Parties, prestigious Corporate Events, and milestone Birthday Parties, our hospitality team ensures flawless execution.
